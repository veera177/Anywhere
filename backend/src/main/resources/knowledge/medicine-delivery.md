# Medicine delivery

## How customers access Medicine Delivery

Open the Medicine Delivery section after selecting a district in your profile. The app lists pharmacies whose registered location falls within the configured service radius for your delivery address. Tap a pharmacy card to browse its available medicines, or use the search bar to find a specific medicine by name across all pharmacies in the district.

> **Important:** Anywhere's Medicine Delivery is a platform for ordering medicines from registered local pharmacies. It does not provide medical advice, diagnose conditions, or recommend dosages. Please consult a qualified healthcare professional for all medical guidance.

## Searching medicine products

Use the search bar to type a medicine name (for example, "Paracetamol", "Dolo 650", "Ibuprofen"). The backend returns matching products from active pharmacies within your district and service radius. Each result shows the medicine name, dosage/pack details, price, and the pharmacy stocking it.

If no results appear, the medicine may not be stocked by any active pharmacy in your district, or all matching products are currently out of stock. Contact your local pharmacy directly for urgently needed medications.

## Checking current product availability

Medicine availability is live and managed by pharmacy partners. A product is only displayed if the pharmacy has marked it as currently in stock. Availability can change at any time. The AI Assistant retrieves availability from the live backend — it does not store or guess whether a specific medicine is in stock.

If a medicine you need is not listed, try searching by generic name or active ingredient. If it remains unavailable, contact the pharmacy directly or visit in person for time-sensitive needs.

## Prescription upload and verification

Certain medicines classified as prescription-only require a valid doctor's prescription before the order can be confirmed.

**How the prescription process works:**

1. When you add a prescription-only medicine to your cart, the system prompts you to upload a prescription.
2. Accepted file formats: JPG, PNG, and PDF. Maximum file size: 5 MB.
3. The uploaded prescription is reviewed by the pharmacy partner (Anywhere uses a simulated pharmacist review for verification).
4. A prescription that has been verified remains valid for repeat orders for **30 days**. After 30 days, a new prescription must be uploaded.
5. Blurry, illegible, or mismatched prescriptions are rejected. Upload a clear, legible scan or photo.

The AI Assistant will direct you through the prescription upload step but cannot approve or verify prescriptions itself — this is handled by the pharmacy partner.

**Note:** Prescription verification rules are governed by the actual application configuration. If the process works differently in the live application, the application behaviour takes precedence.

## Checkout

After adding medicines to the cart:

1. If prescription-only items are present, complete the prescription upload and wait for approval (or proceed if a valid prescription is already on file).
2. Proceed to Checkout.
3. The backend revalidates availability and current prices.
4. Confirm your delivery address is within the 25 km medicine service radius of the pharmacy.
5. Review the itemised total, delivery fee, and any discount.
6. Choose a payment method and confirm.

An order is placed only after the backend confirms it.

## District and pharmacy availability

Pharmacies are registered to a specific district. Only pharmacies in the selected district appear. Your delivery address must fall within the pharmacy's 25 km service radius. If no pharmacies appear, check your district setting and delivery address.

The default medicine service radius is 25 km from each pharmacy's registered location. Actual radius values come from live platform settings and may differ from documentation.

## Common errors and troubleshooting

| Error | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| No pharmacies found | No active pharmacy in district | Check district and address |
| Medicine not found | Not stocked in district | Search by generic name; try another pharmacy |
| Prescription rejected | Blurry image or medicine mismatch | Re-upload a clear prescription |
| Delivery not available | Address outside 25 km radius | Use a closer delivery address |
| Item unavailable at checkout | Went out of stock after adding to cart | Remove and search for an alternative |

## Frequently asked customer questions

**Q: Does Anywhere provide medical advice?**
A: No. Anywhere is a delivery platform. For medical advice, dosage guidance, or treatment recommendations, please consult a qualified healthcare professional or visit a licensed physician.

**Q: Why can't I find a specific medicine?**
A: The medicine may not be stocked by pharmacies in your district, or it may be temporarily out of stock. Try the generic name. For urgent needs, contact a pharmacy directly.

**Q: Do I always need a prescription?**
A: Only for medicines classified as prescription-only. Over-the-counter medicines can be ordered without a prescription. The app will prompt you if a prescription is needed when you attempt to check out.

**Q: How long does my prescription stay valid on Anywhere?**
A: A verified prescription remains valid for 30 days for repeat orders of the same medicines. After 30 days, upload a fresh prescription.

**Q: Can the AI tell me if a medicine is safe for me?**
A: No. The AI Assistant can help you find and order medicines available on the platform, but it cannot provide medical advice, assess drug interactions, or recommend dosages. Please consult your doctor or pharmacist.
