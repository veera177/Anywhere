## Refund policy
Q: What is your refund policy?
A: Refunds are processed back to your original payment method within 5-7 business days for cancelled or missing items. For COD orders, refund is issued to your verified UPI ID or bank account.
Category: ORDERS

## Order cancellation
Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: You can cancel your order only while it is in ORDER_PLACED or RESTAURANT_ACCEPTED status. Once preparation starts or the order is out for delivery, cancellation is not permitted.
Category: ORDERS

## Out of stock items
Q: What happens if an item is out of stock?
A: If an item is out of stock at the partner store, our support team contacts you to offer an alternative item or instantly cancels that item with a full refund to your payment method.
Category: ORDERS

## Food delivery fee and free delivery
Q: What is the delivery fee and free delivery threshold for food?
A: Food delivery is free on orders above Rs 199. For orders below Rs 199, tiered rates apply: Tier 1 base Rs 35, Tier 2 base Rs 25, Tier 3 base Rs 20 (first 3 km included). Max fee Rs 65-100.
Category: FEES

## Grocery delivery fee and free delivery
Q: What is the delivery fee and free delivery threshold for groceries?
A: Grocery delivery is free on orders above Rs 299. Below Rs 299, tiered distance rates apply: Tier 1 base Rs 28, Tier 2 base Rs 22, Tier 3 base Rs 18 with 3 km base distance. Max fee Rs 65-100.
Category: FEES

## Medicine delivery fee and free delivery
Q: What is the delivery fee and free delivery threshold for medicine?
A: Medicine delivery is free on orders above Rs 149. Below Rs 149, tiered fees apply: Tier 1 base Rs 22, Tier 2 base Rs 17, Tier 3 base Rs 13 for 3 km. Emergency priority adds Rs 15 surcharge.
Category: FEES

## Courier service fee
Q: What is the delivery fee for courier service?
A: Courier service has no free delivery threshold. Tier 1 base fee is Rs 25, Tier 2 is Rs 20, and Tier 3 is Rs 15 for the first 3 km. Max fee is Rs 65 to Rs 100 based on tier.
Category: FEES

## Food delivery service radius
Q: What is the service radius for food delivery?
A: Food delivery has a maximum service radius of 15 km from the restaurant partner to ensure dishes arrive hot and fresh.
Category: DELIVERY

## Grocery delivery service radius
Q: What is the service radius for grocery delivery?
A: Grocery delivery has an operating service radius of up to 25 km from the partner grocery store to your delivery location.
Category: DELIVERY

## Medicine delivery service radius
Q: What is the service radius for medicine delivery?
A: Medicine delivery operates up to a 25 km radius from the partner pharmacy to your address.
Category: DELIVERY

## Courier service radius
Q: What is the service radius for courier service?
A: Courier and parcel delivery connects pickup and drop points across a radius of up to 30 km within the district.
Category: DELIVERY

## Delivery partner payout
Q: How do delivery partners get paid?
A: Delivery partners receive 100% of the delivery fee for the order. In Tier 3 districts, delivery partners receive an extra 8% top-up bonus paid from platform commissions.
Category: PARTNERS

## Food shop commission
Q: What commission does Anywhere charge food restaurants?
A: For food delivery, Anywhere charges restaurant partners a 15% commission on order product total with a minimum commission floor of Rs 15.
Category: PARTNERS

## Grocery store commission
Q: What commission does Anywhere charge grocery stores?
A: For grocery orders, Anywhere charges store partners an 8% commission on product total with a commission floor of Rs 15.
Category: PARTNERS

## Pharmacy commission
Q: What commission does Anywhere charge pharmacies?
A: For medicine orders, Anywhere charges pharmacy partners a 5% commission on product total with a commission floor of Rs 15.
Category: PARTNERS

## Courier platform fee
Q: What platform fee applies to courier bookings?
A: For courier shipments, Anywhere charges a 20% platform service fee with a minimum floor of Rs 15. The remaining distance fee goes directly to the delivery partner.
Category: PARTNERS

## Becoming a shop partner
Q: How can a shop or restaurant become an Anywhere partner?
A: Local restaurants, supermarkets, and pharmacies can apply under Partner Onboarding with FSSAI/drug license, GST details, shop photos, and bank account for automated settlements.
Category: PARTNERS

## Becoming a delivery partner
Q: How can I register as a delivery partner?
A: Drivers with a two-wheeler, valid driving license, vehicle RC, Aadhaar card, and an Android smartphone can apply via the Anywhere Partner portal to start delivering in their district.
Category: PARTNERS

## Prescription verification process
Q: How do prescriptions work?
A: Prescription medicines require uploading a doctor prescription. Verification uses a simulated pharmacist review with a 10-second check validating clarity and medicine match.
Category: MEDICINE

## Prescription repeat orders
Q: How long is a verified prescription valid for repeat orders?
A: Verified prescriptions remain valid for repeat orders for 30 days. After 30 days, a renewed doctor prescription must be uploaded.
Category: MEDICINE

## Prescription file size and formats
Q: What file size and formats are accepted for prescriptions?
A: Uploads accept JPG, PNG, and PDF files up to 5 MB. Files exceeding 5 MB or blurry images are rejected by the review system.
Category: MEDICINE

## Coupons and promo codes
Q: How do coupons work on Anywhere?
A: Enter a valid coupon code at checkout before payment. If eligible, discounts apply instantly to your cart subtotal. Only one coupon can be applied per order.
Category: PAYMENTS

## Districts covered
Q: Which districts are covered by Anywhere?
A: Anywhere serves 27 districts in Tamil Nadu including Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Salem, Karaikudi, Erode, Tirunelveli, Thanjavur, Vellore, and more.
Category: GENERAL

## District tier classification
Q: How are districts classified into tiers?
A: Chennai and Coimbatore are Tier 1; Madurai, Trichy, and Salem are Tier 2; Karaikudi and emerging towns are Tier 3. Tiers govern baseline delivery fees and partner top-ups.
Category: GENERAL

## Saved addresses and Use My Location
Q: How does address saving and Use My Location work?
A: Use My Location uses your device GPS to detect your district and area. You can save multiple addresses like Home and Work under Profile > Saved Addresses for 1-click checkout.
Category: ACCOUNT

## Payment methods supported
Q: What payment options are supported?
A: Anywhere supports secure online payments via Razorpay (UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Credit/Debit cards, Net Banking) as well as Cash on Delivery (COD) for eligible deliveries.
Category: PAYMENTS

## Cash on Delivery policy
Q: Is Cash on Delivery available on all orders?
A: Cash on Delivery is available for food and grocery orders in all active districts. Exact change or UPI scan upon delivery is recommended for smooth handovers.
Category: PAYMENTS

## Account and data privacy
Q: How is my personal data and account privacy protected?
A: Personal profile, saved addresses, and phone numbers are encrypted and never sold to third parties. They are shared only with assigned partners strictly for order fulfillment.
Category: ACCOUNT

## Repeat order feature
Q: How does the Repeat Order feature work?
A: Go to Orders > Previous Orders or tell the AI assistant repeat last order. Available items from your previous order are automatically added back into your cart.
Category: ORDERS
